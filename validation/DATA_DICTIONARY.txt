Source-comparison release data dictionary

Files and record identity
source_adjudication_12.csv contains all 12 sampled trial-family records in their frozen sampling order. Identify a selected record by nct, family and outcome_index; class_index identifies its selected time/class. The public sampling and comparison files preserve the selected records, shared numerical values and scientific classifications while omitting narrative, reviewer and local-document fields. Their public-file hashes and the separate original-file hashes are recorded in source_adjudication_manifest.json. No unavailable record was replaced.

Current comparison fields
source_comparison_status is the completed source-comparison category. It takes one of five values:
CONFIRMED_ADJUSTED_MEAN_INELIGIBLE: matching final results identify adjusted means incompatible with arithmetic change-score standardization; one record.
NUMERIC_FIELDS_CORROBORATED_PARTIAL: selected numerical fields are independently supported, but complete record validity is not established; two records.
NUMERICAL_POPULATION_DISCORDANCE_UNRESOLVED: numerical and analysis-population differences prevent a definitive reconciliation; one record.
NUMERICAL_SUMMARY_UNRESOLVED: contextual documents were recovered but do not resolve the selected final numerical summary; five records.
FINAL_NUMERICAL_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE: an independent final numerical source was not recovered; three records.

confirmed_ineligible_for_arithmetic_sensitivity is a boolean action field. Only NCT01145755, MADRS, outcome_index 0 is true. The added sensitivity excludes that record; it does not replace a mean, SD, sample size or effect direction. All unresolved records remain included under the stated eligibility rules.
source_url and source_locator identify the recovered evidence. source_evidence summarizes the document findings; remaining_uncertainty states what they do not resolve. The private-only local_evidence_files, evidence_sha256 and reviewer columns are absent from this public file. Source PDFs and clinical-study reports are not redistributed.

Preserved pre-comparison placeholders
source_status, independent_source_url, source_page, mean_check, dispersion_check, n_check, arm_check, timepoint_check, population_check and overall_status originated in the frozen sampling worksheet. NOT_YET_CHECKED, PENDING and empty values in those retained columns describe the worksheet before source comparison. Use source_comparison_status, source_evidence, remaining_uncertainty and confirmed_ineligible_for_arithmetic_sensitivity for the completed comparison. The private reviewer field was omitted from the public derivative.

Registry and calculated fields
All preceding registry and analysis columns retain the selected snapshot values and definitions. y is the signed Hedges g contrast, positive toward benefit in the experimental group; v is its working sampling variance. mean_active, mean_placebo, sd_active, sd_placebo, n_active and n_placebo are the selected arithmetic-format inputs, not source-validated replacements. improvement_sign gives the orientation. A numerical value retained in this file is not certified as correct merely because it was not excluded.

Interpretation
Partial corroboration is field-specific. Protocols and statistical analysis plans can establish planned methods without establishing the summary type actually reported. The 12-trial source comparison is a post hoc sample from the strict eligible subset, not an estimate of the full registry's error prevalence.
